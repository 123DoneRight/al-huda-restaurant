The spice route

Four stops, one pot.

Stop 1 of 4

  1. 01Colombo, Sri Lanka

    Cinnamon

    True cinnamon grows in Sri Lanka. Traders shipped the bark across the Indian Ocean to the Horn of Africa.

  2. 02Kochi, India

    Cardamom

    Kerala's hills grow the green pods that now scent Somali tea, rice and sweets.

  3. 03Zanzibar, Tanzania

    Cloves

    Zanzibar became one of the world's great clove growers in the 1800s, and its cloves sailed north.

  4. 04Mogadishu, Somalia

    The pot

    Ports on the Somali coast received spice and rice by sea. Today they meet in one pot.

Coastlines are real. The sea route is illustrative, not a historical record.
